First time? Here's what everyone asks.

Will extensions damage my hair?
No. Wefts are fitted with silicone microbeads, which protect the natural hair. No glue, chemicals, heat, bonding or weaving.
How long does it take?
40 minutes to an hour. A full head takes under an hour and a half head under 40 minutes, plus about 15 minutes to trim and blend.
How often do I come back?
Every 6 to 8 weeks for a re-lift, depending on how fast your hair grows. Re-lifts start at $90.
Can I colour them?
Yes. Virgin hair has never been processed, coloured or chemically treated, so it can be coloured.
Is there a more affordable option?
Our Euro hair: 100% human hair in 18″ or 22″, 5 textures from silky straight to deep curl, and 60+ colour blends. There are three install methods: Euro-Invisi Weft, Mini-Weft and Invisi-Strands.
Which system is right for me?
Any of them may suit you, and every one is gentle. If you're unsure, that's what the $25 consultation is for.
